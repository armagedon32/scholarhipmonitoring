Model Retraining Approval AI Governance Committee Retraining is performed only by authorized personnel after a recorded review verdict of “Needs Retraining”. Every retraining run is audited.

Human Validation Requirement

Human-in-the-loop: authorized personnel (Scholarship Coordinator / AI Governance Committee) must review every AI-generated prediction before any final scholarship action is taken. The prediction is decision-support only, and the responsible reviewer is identified in the Audit Logs.

Appeal & Override Process

  1. Student submits an appeal through the Scholar Dashboard, stating the reason for the dispute.
  2. The appeal is recorded in the Audit Logs and the Coordinator is notified.
  3. The Coordinator (or Administrator) reviews the appeal and decides: Upheld (confirm the prediction) or Overridden (change the status with remarks).
  4. The decision and remarks are recorded in the Audit Logs, and the student is notified.
